About the product

no commas isn’t a habit-tracking journal with goals, achievements and streaks that keep you coming back. It’s a place to get a thought down quickly before it slips away — typos, mistakes, no punctuation and all.

The task

Rewrite the AI-generated English interface copy: make it sound natural and find a voice that fits the idea of the app.

Translate the copy into Russian, following the same principles.

What I did

product voice

Defined how the app talks to people: no pressure, encouraging quick notes rather than perfect ones. The tone is calm, restrained and free of extra words.

glossary

Built a product glossary. The terms keep up the image of a real notebook — pages, covers, stickers — and still read clearly in the interface.

editing

Edited 56 of 305 strings: shortened long phrases, replaced colloquial expressions with precise words and added actions where they were missing. Removed a few strings entirely. Checked that every string fits on screen in both languages.

translation

Translated about 300 strings into natural Russian, in the same voice and with consistent terminology.

How no commas sounds

The no commas voice takes everything extra off a note: demands, judgment and obligations. It holds the copy together in both languages.

messy is fine

A draft, a typo, an unfinished thought — that’s what a note normally looks like. The app’s features and copy support this principle at every step.

let it out, don’t keep a diary

No streaks, goals or praise. The journal is for clearing chaotic thoughts out of your head right now.

like talking

Writing is like talking. Dictating is as easy as typing.

paper, not a screen

The interface talks about the journal as a physical object: notebooks lie on the desk, stickers and photos get stuck on, and so on.
