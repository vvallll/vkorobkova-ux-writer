About the challenge

Daily UX writing tasks, from simple to complex. Each comes with a situation, a format, character limits and an extra rule. Some give enough context, some very little — then I fill in the gaps myself.

The task

Write copy for each task in a tone that fits the product and build the screens in Figma. In complex tasks, first get to the bottom of the problem: suggest hypotheses and the data to test them, and assess the risks of the solution.

What I’m doing

level 1

Basic states: a sign-in error, a failed payment, a push notification, a permission request, an empty screen, form validation.

level 2

Multi-screen flows: restoring unsaved work, canceling a subscription, identity verification, a transfer fee, signing in from a new device, rescheduling a meeting.

level 3

Analysis tasks: abandoned sign-ups, a drop in conversion, too many notifications. For the copy, I keep a table: goal, options, final version.

Examples

A few tasks from different levels: how I approached each one and the copy I ended up with.

error

Identity verification failed

The person has already spent time taking the photo, so the error says exactly what couldn’t be read. If the system doesn’t know, there’s a general version with the same next step.

Не удалось прочитать дату рождения

Мы не смогли прочитать необходимые данные. Сделайте снимок заново, убедившись, что все данные хорошо видны.

Загрузить заново

security

Sign-in from a new device

An email about a sign-in from a new device can’t claim the account was hacked, but it shouldn’t reassure people for nothing either. It says what to do if it wasn’t you, and that nothing is needed if it was. The second version adds one more step: removing unfamiliar devices.

Вход с нового устройства

В ваш аккаунт выполнен вход с нового устройства. Если это были не вы, немедленно смените пароль, чтобы защитить аккаунт. Если это были вы, ничего делать не нужно.

notifications

Complaints about frequent pushes

Users of a language learning app complained about frequent notifications, while the team wanted them to practice every day. I split the pushes into three types and set sending conditions for each. All the notifications keep one tone: warm, informal, with a light touch of humor.
