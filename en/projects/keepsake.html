About the product

Keepsake is an app for private parties, and it looks like a party: wine red and gold, a disco ball, polaroids. All the planning is in one place: date, venue, dress code, guests, playlist, menu and wishlist. After the party, the event turns into a polaroid — a shared album with the guests’ photos.

The task

Build the app from scratch: plan the structure, design the screens and put them together with AI. As a UX writer, write all the interface copy in English and Russian.

What I’m doing

product voice

The app acts like a party companion: it’s casual and warm and isn’t afraid of colloquial phrases — invite your people, want to remember how it was? Still, every line explains what happened and what to do next.

copy from scratch

I write the copy for every screen: from the invitation and the guest’s reply to the empty calendar and notifications about new songs in the playlist.

host and guest

I write for two roles: the host needs tips on setting everything up, while the guest needs only the essentials. For example, a guest sees the full address after replying to the invitation.

glossary

I’m building a product glossary so that parties, guests and memories are called the same thing everywhere, in both languages.
