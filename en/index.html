About me

Texts have always taken up quite a lot of space in my life. I write prose, translate, edit and read about everything under the sun. I work with Russian and English, and I’m curious about how translation works and why the same thought can sound completely different.

Lately, the digital world has been taking up more and more of my attention. Right now I’m deeply involved in UX writing, working with the kind of texts that have become an essential part of how people interact with the world every day.
